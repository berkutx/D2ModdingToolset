4. GLSL shader presets
----------------------

The Shaders directory contains the exact presets exposed by the C4dll-R menu,
from the cnc-ddraw/libretro GLSL shader package:
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders

Copyright and license notices are retained inside the individual shader files.
Most selected presets use MIT or Unlicense terms. The xBRZ multipass preset
also contains GPL-3.0 code and is distributed under GPL-3.0, consistently with
C4dll-R's own license.
